I am a dual PhD candidate at the [School of Hotel and Tourism Management](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/shtm/), The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and [Surrey Business School](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/surrey-business-school), University of Surrey, expected to finish in 2027. My supervisors are Dr Fei Hao and Dr Neil Hengyun Li at PolyU, and Dr Erin Chao Ling and Dr Jason Li Chen at Surrey.

I study how digital technology and public policy change tourism and hospitality markets. I work with three kinds of tools: causal inference for policies that roll out in stages, time-series models, and large language models used as measurement instruments for text, validated before the results are trusted. My PhD looks at how self-service technology changes hotel operations and the guest experience. In 2026 our papers won Best Paper Awards at IATE and CHME.

Before the PhD I earned an MSc in Applied Statistics from Shanghai University of International Business and Economics, a BEcon in Finance from Shanghai University, and a Bachelor of Business with Distinction from the University of Technology Sydney.
